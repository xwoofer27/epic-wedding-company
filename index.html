Our Story

Eight years. Three hundred love stories. One uncompromising vision.

Born from a belief that every love story deserves to be remembered exactly as it felt.

Eternal Weddings was founded in 2016 by Elena Rossi, a fine art photographer who spent her early career documenting the quiet, unrepeatable moments of destination weddings across Europe and Asia. What began as a single camera and a deep reverence for the art of storytelling has grown into one of the most sought-after wedding photography studios in the world.

We do not simply take photographs. We preserve the weight of a father's glance, the tremor in a mother's hands, the laughter that erupts when old friends reunite after years apart. We capture the spaces between the scheduled moments — the ones that no one planned, but everyone will remember.

From the sun-drenched villas of Tuscany to the palaces of Rajasthan, from intimate elopements on the Amalfi Coast to grand celebrations in Paris and beyond, our work follows love wherever it leads. Every couple who trusts us with their story becomes part of ours.
